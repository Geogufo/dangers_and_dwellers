# Contributing

Use an issue or pull request to propose a rule clarification, report a card correction, or share a playtest result.

## Reporting a rule or card issue

Include the file, page, exact card title or rulebook section, what happened, and the proposed wording. Keep proposed rules separate from confirmed rules until the authors decide how to incorporate them.

## Reporting a playtest

Record the number of stakeholders or teams, roles used, starting difficulty, Event sequence, final island values and treasury, medals earned, and the point at which play ended. Briefly describe decisions that felt unclear or had an unexpected outcome.

## Updating materials

- Keep the extended and short rulebooks consistent.
- Use the original hazard and health symbols as well as the statistic labels.
- Retain the source PDF files unless an updated source replaces them. Record replacements in the source manifest and changelog.
- Render changed DOCX files and inspect every page before submitting them.
- Check relative links and image paths when editing Markdown.
- Preserve author credits and the distinction between card fronts, portrait variants and printed backs.
